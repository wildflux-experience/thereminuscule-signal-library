# Signal : `saline-solution-bioimpedance-1khz`

This dataset contains raw measurements produced by a specialized electronic device. The device measured the bio-impedance of saltwater solutions with different salt concentrations, from 9 g/L to 40 g/L, at a frequency of 1 kHz.

Bio-impedance refers to the way a biological material reacts to a small electrical signal. This electrical response can provide useful information about its chemical composition.

*Source: LIRMM (CNRS - University of Montpellier) | Thesis: https://theses.hal.science/tel-02138957*
