# Signal : `albacore-epigenetic-clock`

The data come from biological samples collected from wild albacore tuna caught in the western Indian Ocean, especially around Réunion Island, the Seychelles, and South Africa between 2013 and 2014.

For each tuna, a small tissue sample was used to extract DNA and measure chemical signals found on the genome: methylation marks. The methylation matrix can be seen as the fish molecular identity card.

*Source: COOOL / dataset : https://github.com/ThomasCOOOL/Albacore_epigenetic_clock / article : 10.1007/s11357-026-02192-0*
