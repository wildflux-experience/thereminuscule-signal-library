# Signal : `dcp-urelease-018`

Atmospheric pressure and air temperature recorded at sea level by a Fish Aggregating Device (FAD) equipped with a *urelease* tag.

The FAD is located off the coast of Saint-Paul, Réunion Island.

The recording started on 2024-09-11 and spans approximately 18 days.

*Source: Ifremer DOI / LIRMM (CNRS – University of Montpellier) – 2024*
